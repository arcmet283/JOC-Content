TOP STORY THIS WEEK:
Cornelious "Quad" Brown IV - Alabama A&M University
Cornelious Brown IV is playing his EIGHTH season of college football — a medical-redshirt/eligibility odyssey that started at Georgia State, continued at UT Martin, and has landed him at Alabama A&M, where teammates and the HBCU world now call him "Unc." Through four games in 2026 he's thrown for 1,111 yards and 8 touchdowns at a 67.4% completion rate, leading Alabama A&M to a 3-1 start heading into their first SWAC matchup of the season against Florida A&M. He's on the HBCU National Player of the Year Watch List, and his longevity story is genuinely unlike anything else in the sport right now — nobody else in HBCU football, or college football period, has bounced across three programs and eight seasons to land as a Player of the Year candidate at 25+ years old.
Hook angle: "He's been in college football since before some of his teammates started high school. And he might be the best quarterback in the SWAC right now."
Audience this serves: 25-54 (longevity/journeyman narrative resonates broadly; "Unc" framing pulls in a slightly older, culturally-aware audience)
