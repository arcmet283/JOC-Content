TOP STORY THIS WEEK:
Sahfi Reed — University of Houston to Lincoln University (PA)

Sahfi Reed is a Philadelphia native who starred at Martin Luther King High School as both a wide receiver/defensive back and an All-American sprinter, drawing Division I football offers from Syracuse, UConn, Temple, Akron, and Old Dominion before choosing to walk a dual-sport path at the University of Houston, a Power Four (Big 12) program. At Houston he saw only limited snaps at wide receiver but thrived on the track, earning USTFCCCA Second Team All-American honors in 2025 as part of Houston's 4x400m relay team, which qualified for the NCAA Division I Outdoor Championships in Eugene, Oregon (season-best 3:03.49, among the fastest in program history). In January 2026, instead of re-entering the portal for another Power Four depth chart, Reed committed to Lincoln University (PA) — a Division II HBCU in the CIAA with a legendary track program (17 NCAA national championships, the namesake inspiration for the Track Phi Track fraternity). He arrives with three years of football eligibility, verified track speed (100m: 10.89, 200m: 21.67, 400m: 46.14), and has already posted a 21.85-second indoor 200m at Lincoln — one of the more competitive HBCU times this season. He is expected to compete for Lincoln's football team for the first time in the 2026 season, which is beginning as this report is written (Week 0/1 games Aug 27-29).

Hook angle: "A Big 12 program couldn't find snaps for him. A Division II HBCU is about to get both a football playmaker and an All-American sprinter in one player."

Audience this serves: 18-34 (recruiting/portal-obsessed college football audience), plus HBCU track and field fans — a demographic JOC hasn't tapped much yet

Title format suggestion: Transfer / "What Happened?" format — "From Houston to a Division II HBCU! What Happened?" (or name Lincoln University specifically in the title per the proven formula: both schools named, no vague "Power 4" placeholder)

2. "The Only One Drafted: Carson Vinson's Path from Alabama A&M to the NFL" — Draft/Advocacy
Story: Carson Vinson was the only HBCU player selected in the entire 2025 NFL Draft, taken by the Baltimore Ravens in the 5th round (No. 141 overall) — the first Alabama A&M player drafted since 2011 and the first offensive player from the program drafted in more than 30 years. At 6'7", 320 lbs, Vinson started all 48 games of his college career, earned AFCA FCS All-America Second Team honors, was named SWAC Offensive Lineman of the Year, and was the only HBCU player invited to both the 2025 NFL Combine and the Reese's Senior Bowl. He's now entering his second NFL season with the Ravens in 2026.
Why this works for JOC: Directly continues the advocacy lane (overlooked HBCU talent breaking through) with a genuine draft success story rather than another "shutout" narrative — a hopeful counterpoint given 2026 saw zero HBCU players drafted for the second time in three years.
Hook angle: "In a draft with zero HBCU picks the following year, one small-school lineman from Alabama A&M had already broken a 30-year drought."

3. "The Hall of Famer's Son Who Chose an HBCU" — Eden James / Howard University — Current Story

Now a senior, James has quietly put together 1,488 rushing yards and seven touchdowns across four years at Howard. The Bison are rebuilding under new head coach and alumnus Ted White after a 5-7 finish in 2025, and James is positioned as the foundation of that turnaround entering the 2026 season, which opened at the MEAC/SWAC Challenge against Alabama A&M.
