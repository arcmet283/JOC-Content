TOP STORY THIS WEEK:
LEMAR PARRISH - Lincoln University (Missouri)

Lemar Parrish grew up in Winter Haven, Florida, and attended Lincoln University in Jefferson City, Missouri — a small HBCU with no football reputation — where he became a four-year letterman and 1969 All-American cornerback and return specialist. The Cincinnati Bengals drafted him in the 7th round of the 1970 NFL Draft. What followed was one of the most underrated careers in NFL history: Parrish made 8 Pro Bowls (1970, 1971, 1974–1977, 1979, 1980) — the same number as Deion Sanders — along with 3x First-team All-Pro honors (1976, 1979, 1980), 47 career interceptions, 13 return touchdowns, and an NFL-leading 18.8 yards per punt return average in 1974. He never missed a start in 13 NFL seasons, playing for the Bengals (1970–1977), Washington (1978–1982), and Buffalo (1982). The Bengals themselves called him "Deion Before Deion." He is in the Black College Football Hall of Fame (2023), the NBCA Hall of Fame (2024), and was added to the Cincinnati Bengals Ring of Honor in 2025. As of 2026, he has never been inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame — despite credentials that match or exceed several corners already enshrined in Canton.

Hook angle: Open with the question: "How many Pro Bowls does it take to get into Canton?" — then reveal that Deion Sanders made 8, and so did Lemar Parrish. Only one of them is in the Hall of Fame. Tell the story of a 7th-round pick from a small Missouri HBCU who became one of the best corners in NFL history — and still can't get through Canton's door.
Audience this serves: 35–54 (classic era, recognizes Deion-era football, personally invested in HOF justice debates); also 25–44 (advocacy angle, systemic recognition argument)
Title format suggestion: Comparison + Advocacy — "He Had As Many Pro Bowls As Deion Sanders. Why Isn't He in the Hall of Fame?" OR "The Greatest HBCU Corner You Never Heard Of: What Happened to Lemar Parrish" OR "Deion Before Deion: The 8-Pro-Bowl Corner From a Missouri HBCU Who Still Isn't in Canton"
