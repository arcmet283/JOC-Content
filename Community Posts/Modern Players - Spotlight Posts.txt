POST 1 — Michael Strahan | Texas Southern University
------------------------------------------------------
📸 Photo: Michael Strahan in New York Giants uniform

Michael Strahan. Texas Southern University.

He played at Texas Southern — an HBCU in Houston — before the New York Giants drafted him in the 2nd round of the 1993 NFL Draft. Over 15 seasons he became the most feared pass rusher of his generation. In 2001 he set the NFL single-season sack record with 22.5 — a record that has stood for over two decades.

He earned 7 Pro Bowl selections, won Super Bowl XLII in February 2008, and was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2014.

Texas Southern University. The school that built the man who owns the sack record. Remember that.


POST 2 — Shaquille Leonard | South Carolina State University
------------------------------------------------------------
📸 Photo: Shaquille Leonard in Indianapolis Colts uniform

Shaquille Leonard. South Carolina State University.

The Indianapolis Colts drafted him in the 2nd round of the 2018 NFL Draft and he immediately changed their defense. In his rookie season alone he recorded 163 tackles and was named NFL Defensive Rookie of the Year. He went on to earn 3 First-Team All-Pro selections and became one of the most dominant linebackers in the NFL.

South Carolina State has now produced Harry Carson, Donnie Shell, and Shaquille Leonard — three of the most accomplished linebackers and defensive players in NFL history across three different generations.

One HBCU. Three generations of dominance. The standard never dropped.


POST 3 — Tarik Cohen | North Carolina A&T State University
----------------------------------------------------------
📸 Photo: Tarik Cohen in Chicago Bears uniform

Tarik Cohen. North Carolina A&T State University.

Every team passed on him in the 2017 NFL Draft. Every single one. The Chicago Bears signed him as an undrafted free agent and in his first season he became one of the most explosive playmakers in the NFL — 370 rushing yards, 53 receptions, and a presence that changed how defenses had to game plan for Chicago. In 2018 he made the Pro Bowl.

At 5'6 and 181 pounds, he was told for years he was too small to play at the next level. North Carolina A&T disagreed. And so did every defense that had to chase him across the field on Sunday.


POST 4 — Rashean Mathis | Bethune-Cookman University
----------------------------------------------------
📸 Photo: Rashean Mathis in Jacksonville Jaguars uniform

Rashean Mathis. Bethune-Cookman University.

He went undrafted in 2003. The Jacksonville Jaguars took a chance on him and he spent 13 seasons in the NFL becoming one of the most consistent cornerbacks of his era. He intercepted 38 passes across his career and earned 4 Pro Bowl selections.

In 2005 he suffered a serious knee injury that ended his season early. He came back in 2006 and was named the AP Comeback Player of the Year. An HBCU cornerback. Undrafted. Four Pro Bowls. A Comeback Player of the Year award. Bethune-Cookman built every bit of that.


POST 5 — Emanuel Wilson | Fort Valley State University
-----------------------------------------------------
📸 Photo: Emanuel Wilson in Green Bay Packers uniform

Emanuel Wilson. Fort Valley State University.

He was not drafted in 2023. Not one of the 259 picks. The Green Bay Packers signed him as an undrafted free agent and he made the most of every opportunity. In 2024 he rushed for 502 yards and 4 touchdowns on 103 carries — averaging 4.87 yards per carry, which ranked 8th in the entire NFL among running backs with at least 100 carries.

Over three seasons in Green Bay he totaled 1,083 rushing yards and 7 touchdowns. In March 2026 the Seattle Seahawks saw enough and signed him as a free agent.

Fort Valley State. Division II. No ESPN coverage. No five-star recruiting. Just a player who proved that the logo on the helmet never determined the talent underneath it.
