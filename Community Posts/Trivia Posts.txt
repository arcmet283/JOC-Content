JOC COMMUNITY TRIVIA POSTS
==========================
Format: "Who" question with 4 player name choices. Answer revealed at the bottom of the post.
Add a player photo to each post — use college uniform when possible to make it less obvious.
Post 2-3x per week between video drops.


POST 1
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who is the only man in history to win an Olympic gold medal AND a Super Bowl ring — and came from an HBCU?

A) Jerry Rice
B) Lynn Swann
C) Bob Hayes
D) John Stallworth

Answer: C — Bob Hayes. Florida A&M. In 1964 he won Olympic gold in the 100-meter dash in Tokyo. Eight years later in January 1972 he won Super Bowl VI with the Dallas Cowboys. The same man. Two of the biggest stages in the world. And he came from an HBCU.


POST 2
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who was the NFL's all-time leading rushing record holder at the time of his retirement — and played college ball at an HBCU?

A) Emmitt Smith
B) Eric Dickerson
C) Barry Sanders
D) Walter Payton

Answer: D — Walter Payton. Jackson State. He played for Jackson State from 1971 to 1974 before the Chicago Bears drafted him 4th overall in 1975. By the time he retired after the 1987 season he held the all-time rushing record with 16,726 yards. And most people still don't know where he went to college.


POST 3
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who was the first Black quarterback to start and WIN a Super Bowl — and came from an HBCU?

A) Warren Moon
B) Doug Williams
C) Randall Cunningham
D) James Harris

Answer: B — Doug Williams. Grambling State. On January 31, 1988 he started Super Bowl XXII for the Washington Redskins and threw for 340 yards and 4 touchdowns in a single quarter. He was named Super Bowl MVP. He came from Grambling State. HBCU built that.


POST 4
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who is this Pro Football Hall of Famer that played his entire college career at South Carolina State?

A) Lawrence Taylor
B) Carl Banks
C) Harry Carson
D) Gary Reasons

Answer: C — Harry Carson. He played at South Carolina State from 1972 to 1975 before joining the New York Giants in 1976. Over 13 seasons he earned 9 Pro Bowl selections and became one of the most dominant linebackers of his era. In 2006 he was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame. SC State built that.


POST 5
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who caught 112 touchdowns in college at an HBCU — and went on to become the greatest receiver to ever play the game?

A) Randy Moss
B) Andre Rison
C) Cris Carter
D) Jerry Rice

Answer: D — Jerry Rice. Mississippi Valley State. From 1980 to 1984 he played for the Delta Devils and put up numbers nobody had ever seen at any level. The San Francisco 49ers drafted him 16th overall in 1985. By the time he retired in 2004 he held virtually every receiving record in NFL history. The GOAT started at an HBCU.


POST 6
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who was the first HBCU player ever taken #1 overall in the NFL Draft — out of Grambling State?

A) Deacon Jones
B) Alan Page
C) Willie Davis
D) Buck Buchanan

Answer: D — Buck Buchanan. In 1963 the Kansas City Chiefs selected him first overall in the AFL Draft making him the first HBCU player ever taken at that spot. He played for the Chiefs from 1963 to 1975 and was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1990. Grambling State put him there.


POST 7
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who is the Hall of Fame tight end that came from a D2 HBCU and won three Super Bowl rings?

A) Tony Gonzalez
B) Antonio Gates
C) Shannon Sharpe
D) Rob Gronkowski

Answer: C — Shannon Sharpe. Savannah State. He played for the Tigers from 1986 to 1990 before going undrafted and signing with the Denver Broncos. He won Super Bowl XXXII in January 1998, Super Bowl XXXIII in January 1999, and Super Bowl XXXV in January 2001 with the Baltimore Ravens. Three rings. From a D2 HBCU.


POST 8
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college or UFL uniform)

Who went undrafted from an HBCU, tore his ACL, got cut by an NFL team — and still came back to win a professional football championship MVP?

A) James Proche
B) Caylin Newton
C) Anthony Brown
D) Ian Wheeler

Answer: D — Ian Wheeler. Howard University. He went undrafted in 2024, signed with the Chicago Bears, and tore his ACL in the preseason before ever playing a regular season snap. The Bears released him in September 2025. He signed with the Louisville Kings of the UFL and on June 13, 2026 he ran for 81 yards and a touchdown in the UFL Championship Game. Louisville won 27-20. Ian Wheeler was named MVP. The road was not straight — but he made it.


POST 9
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who was the first defensive player in NFL history to win Super Bowl MVP — and came from an HBCU?

A) Mike Singletary
B) Wilber Marshall
C) Dan Hampton
D) Richard Dent

Answer: D — Richard Dent. Tennessee State. On January 26, 1986 the Chicago Bears destroyed the New England Patriots 46-10 in Super Bowl XX. Richard Dent had 1.5 sacks, 2 forced fumbles, and a pass deflection that led to a pick. He became the first defensive player in Super Bowl history to win MVP. Tennessee State built him. In 2011 he was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame.


POST 10
--------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who was this NFL co-MVP quarterback that spent his entire college career at an HBCU — and most people have no idea?

A) Daunte Culpepper
B) Michael Vick
C) Donovan McNabb
D) Steve McNair

Answer: D — Steve McNair. Alcorn State. He played for the Braves from 1991 to 1994 and became one of the most productive quarterbacks in college football history before the Houston Oilers took him 3rd overall in the 1995 NFL Draft. In 2003 he shared NFL co-MVP honors with Peyton Manning. One of the most underrated quarterbacks of his generation. And he built every bit of that foundation at an HBCU.
