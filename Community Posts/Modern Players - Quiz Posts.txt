POST 1
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who holds the NFL's single-season sack record — and played college football at an HBCU?

A) Dwight Freeney
B) DeMarcus Ware
C) Michael Strahan
D) Reggie White

Answer: C — Michael Strahan. Texas Southern University. In 2001 he recorded 22.5 sacks in a single season — a record that still stands today. He played 15 seasons for the New York Giants, won Super Bowl XLII in February 2008, earned 7 Pro Bowl selections, and was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2014. Texas Southern built the man who owns the most important record in pass rushing history.


POST 2
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who won NFL Defensive Rookie of the Year in 2018 and became a 3x First-Team All-Pro — straight out of an HBCU?

A) Roquan Smith
B) Micah Parsons
C) Bobby Wagner
D) Shaquille Leonard

Answer: D — Shaquille Leonard. South Carolina State University. He was drafted by the Indianapolis Colts in the 2nd round of the 2018 NFL Draft and immediately became one of the most dominant linebackers in the league. In his rookie season he recorded 163 tackles and was named NFL Defensive Rookie of the Year. He went on to earn 3 First-Team All-Pro selections. South Carolina State has now produced two Pro Football Hall of Famers and one of the best linebackers of his generation. The HBCU pipeline is real.


POST 3
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who went undrafted from an HBCU in 2017 — and made the Pro Bowl the very next season?

A) Darren Sproles
B) Cordarrelle Patterson
C) Tarik Cohen
D) Isaiah McKenzie

Answer: C — Tarik Cohen. North Carolina A&T State University. Every team passed on him in the 2017 NFL Draft. The Chicago Bears signed him as an undrafted free agent. In his first season he rushed for 370 yards, caught 53 passes, and instantly became one of the most electric playmakers in the NFL. In 2018 he made the Pro Bowl. They called him the Human Joystick. North Carolina A&T built him from the ground up.


POST 4
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who is the 4x Pro Bowl cornerback that came from an HBCU and won the AP Comeback Player of the Year award in 2006?

A) Darrelle Revis
B) Nnamdi Asomugha
C) DeAngelo Hall
D) Rashean Mathis

Answer: D — Rashean Mathis. Bethune-Cookman University. He went undrafted in 2003 and signed with the Jacksonville Jaguars. Over 13 NFL seasons he intercepted 38 passes and earned 4 Pro Bowl selections. After a serious knee injury in 2005 he came back in 2006 and was named AP Comeback Player of the Year. Bethune-Cookman sent an undrafted cornerback to four Pro Bowls. That story never gets told enough.


POST 5
-------
📸 Photo: Action shot (college uniform preferred)

Who was the only HBCU player drafted in the entire 2025 NFL Draft — out of 257 picks?

A) Emanuel Wilson
B) Andrew Body
C) Carson Vinson
D) Shedeur Sanders

Answer: C — Carson Vinson. Alabama A&M University. Out of 257 picks in the 2025 NFL Draft, his name was the only one called from an HBCU. One player. One school. In the entire draft. He was selected in the fourth round and became a symbol of both the progress and the problem — proof that HBCU talent can reach the NFL, and proof that the league still isn't looking hard enough to find it.
