Who caught the game-winning touchdown pass in Super Bowl XIV, won four Super Bowl rings with the Pittsburgh Steelers, and started his career at an HBCU?

A) Lynn Swann
B) John Stallworth
C) Louis Lipps
D) Hines Ward

Answer: B — John Stallworth. Alabama A&M. Drafted in the 4th round in 1974, he caught 537 passes for 8,723 yards and 63 touchdowns over his career — all Steelers records at the time of his retirement. In Super Bowl XIV in January 1980 he caught the go-ahead 73-yard touchdown pass that sealed the win over the Los Angeles Rams. He was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2002. Alabama A&M built a champion.

Who set the NFL record for career interception return touchdowns with 9 — including an NFL record 4 in a single season in 1971 — after playing at an HBCU?

A) Ken Houston
B) Emlen Tunnell
C) Dick "Night Train" Lane
D) Rod Woodson

Answer: A — Ken Houston. Prairie View A&M. Drafted in the 9th round in 1967 by the Houston Oilers, he went on to intercept 49 passes over 14 seasons with Houston and Washington, returning 9 of them for touchdowns — an NFL record at the time. His 4 pick-six returns in 1971 set a single-season record that has only been matched twice since. He made 12 straight Pro Bowl/All-Star selections and was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1986. Prairie View A&M built a record breaker.
