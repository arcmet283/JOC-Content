Who caught the go-ahead 73-yard touchdown pass in Super Bowl XIV — a play that still stands as the Super Bowl record for yards per catch in a single game — and played college ball at an HBCU?

A) Lynn Swann
B) John Stallworth
C) Cliff Branch
D) Drew Pearson

Answer: B — John Stallworth. Alabama A&M. On January 20, 1980, he hauled in a 73-yard touchdown from Terry Bradshaw in the fourth quarter of Super Bowl XIV that put the Steelers ahead for good in a 31-19 win over the Rams — a performance that still holds the Super Bowl record for yards per catch in a game (40.33). He retired with 537 catches, 8,723 yards, and 63 touchdowns, all Steelers records at the time, and won four Super Bowl rings. He was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2002. Alabama A&M built a champion.

Who became the first Black head coach of the modern NFL era in 1989, years after starring as a Hall of Fame offensive tackle — and played college ball at an HBCU?

A) Tony Dungy
B) Dennis Green
C) Art Shell
D) Ray Rhodes

Answer: C — Art Shell. Maryland Eastern Shore. On October 3, 1989, the Los Angeles Raiders named him head coach, making him the first Black head coach of the modern NFL era. As a player, he anchored the Raiders' offensive line from 1968 to 1982, won Super Bowl XI and Super Bowl XV, and was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1989. He went on to lead the Raiders to three playoff appearances as head coach. Maryland Eastern Shore built a trailblazer on both sides of the sideline.
