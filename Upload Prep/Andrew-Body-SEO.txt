== DESCRIPTION ==

Notre Dame called. Penn State called. Florida, UCF, and Vanderbilt called. Thirty-six schools in total reached out to Andrew Body the moment he hit the transfer portal. And he turned every single one of them down — to go back to Alabama State.

Body arrived at Texas Southern as a Texas high school legend — over 13,000 career passing yards, 91 touchdowns, third all-time in Texas high school passing history. He set TSU's single-season total offense record as a true freshman before injuries and a coaching change sent him looking for a new home. He landed at Alabama State, and after a shoulder injury cut his debut season short, he came back in 2025 and put together one of the most efficient seasons in HBCU football: over 70% completion, 1,770 passing yards, 20 touchdowns, and just 1 interception in only eight games, plus 518 rushing yards and four more scores on the ground. SWAC Offensive Player of the Year. National HBCU Player of the Year. And Alabama State's first 10-win season since 2004 — the same year Tarvaris Jackson led the Hornets to that mark before becoming the last Alabama State quarterback ever drafted.

Then the portal opened, the Power 4 offers rolled in, and Andrew Body said no to all of them.
